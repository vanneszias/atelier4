# Atelier 4 visual direction

Atelier 4 should feel like an art-night poster brought to life: expressive, welcoming, and carefully made. It brings the energy of Kunstenkamp into a year-round arts community centred on Jesus.

The reference is the owner's Kunstenkamp site, studied on 6 October 2026. Its strongest ideas are large typographic compositions, blocks of confident colour, and a photographic microphone crossing their boundaries. The Atelier 4 brand guide supplies raspberry (#E02062), cobalt (#3546E8), ink (#20201E), paper (#F6F3EA), and the existing four-shape mark. Yellow and orange remain supporting colours from that mark.

## Design decisions

- Compose the homepage as a full-width raspberry typographic poster above an ink invitation and yellow art forms. Retain the short editable CMS heading and copy. A CMS image remains optional.
- Keep the hero focused on typography. Reuse the owner's original microphone cutout, optimised to a 62 KB WebP, only in the song-night artwork.
- Give Muziek, Atelier, Drama, and Dans equally weighted tiles, each in a brand colour. Their numbers describe the four art forms rather than acting as decorative section labels.
- Pace the rest of the page with paper, generous editorial spacing, one featured event, and a yellow invitation. Avoid repeated full-width colour bands.
- Use one bundled Jost font consistently across platforms. Headings are bold and compact; long reading passages are quieter and comfortably spaced.
- Event artwork combines meaningful typography with the microphone or existing brand mark. Event metadata, preparations, and registration status remain real CMS data.
- Inner pages reuse the same type, spacing, button, field, artwork, and footer treatments. Forms retain their consent and server validation behaviour.
- Motion is brief and purposeful: staggered poster entrance, lift on buttons, slight tilt on discipline tiles, and microphone movement on event hover. Honour reduced motion; show all content without JavaScript.
- On narrow screens, stack the poster, omit its large visual, retain the four art-form labels, and keep forms and event details in reading order. The header stays borderless. No section eyebrows or hero footnotes.

## Content and operations

This is a theme update. CMS data, translations, routes, forms, permissions, and infrastructure are preserved. No seed import or schema migration is required. New photography can later replace the CMS hero image without modifying its renderer. Do not invent event dates, attendance numbers, testimonials, or established partner relationships.
